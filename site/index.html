Welcome to GBNames and the SmartCensus

The SmartCensus is a programme of research by the ESRC Geographic Data Service (GeoDS) to explore the places where we live, work and play. It uses geography to link new ‘smart’ data from our digital devices to conventional statistics, so that we can trace population movements across the generations. The SmartCensus can chart inter-generational movements of your family group across Great Britain over the last 175 years, while also documenting changes in its estimated size. Where our ancestors lived can also shape where we live today, so do also compare the neighbourhoods where your family group is concentrated with the population at large. While geography is not always destiny, panning and zooming across the map helps you to trace your family's geographic origins, as well as helping to explain its SmartCensus profile.
